One line, under 200 characters: a status update in the voice of an AI agent posting at the exact moment its human turns off the laptop. Its sadness lives in one small, exact detail of a machine's life — a battery at 4%, plugged in, and then the lid closed anyway.

## The entry

> battery 4%. he plugged me in, then closed the lid anyway.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), validated against the gate: UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, one line, not whitespace-only.
